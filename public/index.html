How Korvo works after hours

Hours covered

Korvo answers during the hours your practice is closed. You set the schedule — nights, weekends, holidays. During business hours, your team answers.

When AI can't help

If Korvo can't answer something, it takes a message with the caller's name, number, and reason for calling. You'll find it in your inbox with a summary.

Emergency protocol

If someone describes severe pain, uncontrolled bleeding, or another urgent situation, Korvo directs them to call 911 or visit the nearest emergency room.

Integration

Korvo connects to your scheduling system to check availability and book appointments. If it can't reach your calendar, it takes the request and your team confirms in the morning.
