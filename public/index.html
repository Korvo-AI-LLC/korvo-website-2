Will callers know it's AI?

Yes, because we tell them. The greeting says it's an AI assistant, and it will say so again if anyone asks. Hiding it wouldn't be honest, and in some states it isn't legal.

People calling at 9 PM aren't hoping for a person. They're hoping not to get voicemail.

Every hour you're closed. Nights, weekends, holidays.

It covers the hours your team is gone, and only those. During the day, your people answer. They're better at it, and we'd rather tell you that now.

What if it can't answer something?

It says so. It won't guess about insurance, prices, or anything clinical. It takes the caller's name, number, and reason, and tells them the office will call back. Every one of those lands in your inbox with a summary.

If it can't reach your schedule, it takes the appointment request and your front desk confirms it in the morning. If someone describes an emergency, it tells them to call 911.
