Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Korvo AI LLC (“Korvo AI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit korvo.ai (the “Site”) and when we provide our AI voice receptionist, SMS messaging, website chat, and related services (the “Services”) to businesses.

1. Who this policy covers

We interact with three groups of people, and our role differs for each:

  • Site visitors — people who browse the Site or submit our consultation form.
  • Clients — the businesses that engage us, and the people who work for them.
  • Callers and customers of our Clients — people who call, text, or chat with a Client business that uses our Services.

If you called or texted a business that uses Korvo AI: we process your information on that business’s behalf and under its instructions. That business decides why and how your information is used, and its own privacy notice applies. For requests about your information, contact the business directly — we will help them respond.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us on the Site

When you request a consultation, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you’re interested in, your preferred meeting time, and the message you write to us.

Information collected automatically on the Site

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server log data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and timestamps. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on the Site.

Client information

When a business engages us, we collect business contact details, account and configuration information (such as business hours, services, FAQs, scheduling rules, and call-handling preferences), access to calendars or other systems the Client chooses to connect, and billing details. Payment card data is handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

Information processed through the Services (“Service Data”)

When a caller or customer interacts with a Client’s AI receptionist, SMS line, or chat, we process information on the Client’s behalf, which may include:

  • Phone number, name, and contact details
  • Details the person provides during the conversation, such as date of birth, the reason for the call, and appointment preferences
  • Call audio recordings, transcripts, chat logs, and AI-generated call summaries
  • Appointment details and SMS message content, delivery status, and replies (including opt-outs)
  • Call metadata such as time, duration, and outcome

For healthcare Clients, Service Data may include protected health information (“PHI”). See Section 6.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to consultation requests and communicate with prospective and current Clients
  • To provide the Services: answering calls, booking appointments, sending confirmations and reminders, summarizing calls, and delivering call logs and reports to Clients
  • To set up, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the quality and reliability of the Services
  • To measure results for Clients, including the metrics used for ROI reporting and billing
  • To bill Clients and manage our business relationship
  • To secure our systems, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We use Service Data only to provide and support the Services for the Client on whose behalf it was collected, as that Client instructs, and as required by law. We do not use Service Data to market our own services to callers.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With the Client: Service Data is delivered to the business the caller or customer contacted.
  • With service providers that help us operate, including cloud hosting and database providers, telephony and SMS carriers, voice AI and language-model providers, email delivery providers, and payment processors. They may use the information only to provide services to us and as their agreements with us allow.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of Korvo AI, our Clients, or others.
  • In a business transfer: as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

5. Text messaging (SMS)

Clients may use our Services to send appointment confirmations, reminders, and replies by text message. Message frequency varies by appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Opt-out requests are honored for the number they are sent from.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as SMS carriers) that deliver the messages.

6. Health information and HIPAA

When a Client is a HIPAA covered entity and we create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on its behalf, we act as that Client’s business associate under a Business Associate Agreement (“BAA”). The BAA governs our handling of that PHI, and if it conflicts with this Policy, the BAA controls.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Site inquiries are kept while we evaluate and manage the business relationship. Service Data, including recordings and transcripts, is kept for the period agreed with the Client or as needed to provide the Services, then deleted or returned at the Client’s direction, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls that limit Service Data to people who need it, and vendors that support HIPAA-appropriate safeguards for healthcare Clients. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify affected parties as required by law and by our agreements.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, and deletion: you may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Site visitors and Clients can contact us directly. Callers should contact the Client business first, since we act on its behalf.
  • Email: you can ask us at any time to stop contacting you.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of certain processing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email hello@korvo.ai. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and you may use an authorized agent where the law allows.

10. Children’s privacy

The Site and Services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site. If you believe a child has given us personal information through the Site, contact us and we will delete it. Information a Client collects about minor patients or customers is handled under that Client’s instructions and applicable law.

11. Where we process information

Korvo AI is based in the United States, and we process and store information in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the U.S., you understand your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date above, and for material changes we’ll provide additional notice to Clients. Continued use of the Site or Services after an update means the updated Policy applies.
