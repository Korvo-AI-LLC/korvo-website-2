BYU students testing practical AI products.

Korvo AI is a small team of students at Brigham Young University. The idea came out of a class at BYU, where we saw how much of the routine work in a small business AI can now handle well.

We wanted to know whether that holds up outside the classroom, so we're testing it. We pick one specific problem, build a focused product for it, and put it in front of real businesses to learn what works.

Our first project is an after-hours voice agent for dental and medical practices. Calls that come in after the front desk closes are easy to measure and costly to miss, which makes them a good first test.

Korvo AI is an independent company and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Brigham Young University.
