About Korvo

Korvo means life. Here is what that means to us, and why we built this company.

Everyone is an artist until the rent is due.

Jack Eliason, founder

You didn't open a practice to answer the phone at 9 PM. You opened it to do work you're good at, and to have a life outside of it. Then the rent came due, and the busywork moved in.

Korvo exists to take the repetitive, soul-draining work off your plate so there's more of you left for the parts that matter. We call that life. It's the whole reason we're here.

  • Creativity

    No dogma

    Fresh eyes on old problems. Nothing is done a certain way just because it always has been.

  • Life

    More of it

    The point of everything we build. Hand the boring, repetitive work to a machine, and get back the hours and the energy to be yourself, create, and go home.

  • Integrity

    Say it. Mean it. Do it.

    Risks before rewards. When we commit, it's done.

  • People

    The whole point

    Not accounts. People, with a business they poured themselves into.
