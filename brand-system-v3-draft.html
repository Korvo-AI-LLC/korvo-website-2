02

The mark

A diamond in four facets: sage and pine for growth, terracotta for the warm, human corner. The geometry doesn't change. How it's handled now has rules.

  • Grid100 × 100 units. Vertices at 50,8 90,50 50,92 10,50. The diamond is 80 wide, 84 tall. That slight height is intentional; don't square it.
  • FacetsTop-left Sage. Top-right Pine. Bottom-left Pine. Bottom-right Terracotta. Never reorder.
  • The crossA split in the ground color, not a drawn line. 3 units at 48px and up; 5 units below 48px so it survives small screens. v2 files used 2 and 6; both retire.
  • Crop for filesUse viewBox="6 4 88 92" so the mark sits tight in its box. The favicon at korvo.ai already does.
  • Wordmark"Korvo" in Playfair Display 700, "AI" in Playfair italic 400. "AI" is Kiln on light grounds, Ember on dark. The gap between mark and wordmark is 0.4× the mark's width.

Four versions, chosen by the ground

Primary on light grounds. Reversed on Pine, Pine Deep, and Teal, where the pine facets turn Cream so the shape doesn't disappear. On Terracotta the terracotta facet would vanish too, so use Mono Cream there. Mono Pine for one-color print.

05

How we talk

A thoughtful friend who happens to know automation. Talk like you're explaining it to a roofer over coffee. That rule from v2 stays the backbone.

Plain, not polished

Short sentences. Real words. If you wouldn't say it out loud across a table, cut it.

Specific over clever

"Live in 72 hours" beats "fast onboarding." Numbers, times, prices, named outcomes.

Honest about tradeoffs

Name the risk before the reward. Say what we don't do. "Not for businesses needing daytime coverage" sells better than it looks.

Warm, never breathless

Exclamation marks are rare. The calm of someone who has solved this before.

Rent, then art New

Lead with the practical promise. Earn one line of life after it. Never the other way around.

Name what's not included New

"No SMS. No chatbots. No websites. One thing, done right." Exclusions are proof of focus.

Say this

"It picks up before the second ring. If it misses a call, you'll see it in the log."

Not this

"Unlock next-level patient engagement with our AI-powered solutions!"

Say this

"Go home at 5. Actually go home."

Not this

"Reclaim your work-life balance and live your best life."

Banned words

  • solutions
  • synergy
  • leverage
  • unlock
  • next-level
  • seamless
  • cutting-edge
  • revolutionize
  • game-changer
  • empower
  • AI-powered (as a selling point)

The first five are from the owner. The rest are proposed. If a sentence needs one of these to work, the sentence is the problem.
